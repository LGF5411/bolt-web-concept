        Questions

        Before your first ride

        How is the fare calculated?

        From the distance and time of the trip and the ride type. The app shows an upfront price once you enter the destination; the map above follows the same idea with example rates and real Tallinn streets.

        Can I book without the app?

        In some cities you can request a ride on the web. Everywhere else the app is needed to book; the route, price and pickup time are shown here first.

        How do I pay?

        Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay in the app. Cash and Bolt Balance depend on the city.

        Is Bolt in my city?

        Bolt works in 850+ cities. The cities list on bolt.eu shows every one.
