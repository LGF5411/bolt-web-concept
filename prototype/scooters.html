      Questions

      Before your first ride

      How much does a ride cost?

      An unlock fee plus a per-minute rate, both set by city. The estimate on the map uses those rates for the real route and speed limits.

      Do I need the app?

      Yes. Scooters and e-bikes unlock with the Bolt app. On a computer, scan the QR code or send yourself a link; on a phone, the button opens the app or the store.

      Where can I park?

      In the green zones on the in-app map. Red zones mean no parking; the ride will not end there.

      What happens in a slow zone?

      The scooter limits its own speed, often to 10 km/h, while you are inside the zone. The display shows the limit as you enter.

      How fast do they go?

      Up to 25 km/h in most cities, lower in slow zones. Beginner mode caps you at 15 km/h for your first five rides.
