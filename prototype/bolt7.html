Meet Bolt 7

Older scooters told you the rules in the app, then fined you after the ride. Bolt 7 shows them on the handlebar while you ride.

  1. 01 · Navigation

    Eyes up, not on your phone.

    Turn-by-turn directions on a full-colour screen, with your speed and the limit for the street you are on.

  2. 02 · Slow zones

    It knows where to slow down.

    Entering a pedestrian or slow zone? The display says so and the limit drops before you get there, not after a warning email.

  3. 03 · On-board AI

    Pavement is for people.

    If the scooter notices it is on the pavement, it warns you first. Keep going, and it slows down by itself.

  4. 04 · Parking

    Park where it is allowed.

    The display highlights parking spots and racks near the end of your trip, so the ride ends tidy and without a fine.

“This approach moves compliance to real-time guidance.”

Ardo Reinsalu, Director of Vehicles at Bolt
