      Frequently asked questions

      What do I need to rent a car?

      A valid driving licence, a verified Bolt account and a payment method. A minimum age or driving experience may apply; the app shows the local rules.

      What kind of cars can I rent?

      Small, electric, medium and premium cars, SUVs and vans, all automatic at no extra cost. The model depends on what is parked near you.

      Can I drive to another city?

      Some cars allow it for an intercity fee. The car card lists where you can end the trip; elsewhere, return the car to where you rented it.

      Can I rent without the app?

      No. Cars unlock only through the Bolt app. This page shows the cars and prices first; the app opens on the car you chose.

      How does reserving a car work?

      Reserve a car for free for a few minutes while you walk to it. After that a small fee may apply.

      How do I pay?

      In the Bolt app, the same way as for a ride or a scooter: card or Apple Pay.
