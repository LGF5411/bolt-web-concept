      How it works

      From the pavement to the driver's seat.

      1. Verify your licence

        Once, in the app. After that every Drive car is open to you.

      2. Find a car nearby

        Every car on the map shows its fuel, range and price before you walk over.

      3. Unlock with your phone

        Hold the button when you are next to the car. The key is inside.

      4. End in a green zone

        Park, leave the key inside, end the trip. Parking there is free.

      What you need

      A valid driving licence, a verified Bolt account and a payment method. Some countries add a minimum age or driving experience.

      Going to another city

      Some cars can end trips in other cities for an intercity fee. The car card lists where; elsewhere, return it to where you started.

      Reserving ahead

      Reserve a car for free for a few minutes while you walk to it. After that a small fee may apply.

      Questions

      Before your first drive

      Is fuel included?

      Yes when you pay by the minute. With a daily rental you pay a fixed fee, and fuel and parking are not included.

      Which cars can I rent?

      Everyday, electric, SUVs, vans and premium cars, all automatic. The exact model depends on what is parked near you.

      Can I book without the app?

      No. Cars unlock only through the Bolt app. On a computer, scan the code or send yourself a link; on a phone, the button opens the app.

      Where can I end my trip?

      In a green zone on the in-app map. Parking there is free. Leave the key inside the car.

      How do I pay?

      In the Bolt app, the same way as for a ride or a scooter: card, Google Pay or Apple Pay.
