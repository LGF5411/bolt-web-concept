          Frequently asked questions

          Can I request a Bolt online?

          In some regions, yes. Everywhere, this page shows the route, pickup time and price first; the app then opens with the trip filled in.

          What determines my ride cost?

          The distance and time of the trip and the ride type. The app shows an upfront price once you enter the destination; the map above uses the same idea with example rates.

          What types of cars are available?

          It depends on the city. In Tallinn: Bolt, Electric, Comfort and XL, all meeting local standards.

          How do I get a ride to or from the airport?

          Request a ride at the arrivals pickup point, or schedule one ahead with your flight. Typical prices from Tallinn Airport are listed above.

          Can I schedule a ride in advance?

          Yes, up to 90 days ahead in the Bolt app.

          What payment methods does Bolt accept?

          Card, Apple Pay or Google Pay in the app. Cash and Bolt Balance depend on the city.

          Is Bolt available in my city?

          Bolt works in 850+ cities. The cities page on bolt.eu lists every one.
